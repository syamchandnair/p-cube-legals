# P Cube Legals

Create a professional website for P Cube Legals, a law office based in Kochi (Cochin), Kerala, practicing before the High Court of Kerala and local Family Courts.

Scope & details:
- Practice: Multi-disciplinary practice handling both trial court (especially Family Courts) and High Court of Kerala matters.
- Core litigation & advisory capabilities:
  1. Legal Research & Analysis: Conducting deep case law analysis for High Court and Family Court matters.
  2. Drafting: Preparing pleadings, petitions, and legal summaries.
  3. Judgment Review: Reviewing judicial decisions to map out key legal reasoning, outcomes, and statutory interpretations.
- Regulatory compliance: Include a Bar Council of India (BCI) Rule 36 compliant informational disclaimer modal/banner on entry.
- Key sections: Practice Areas (High Court Appellate/Writs & Family Court litigation), Advisory & Research Services (Research, Drafting, Judgment Review), About the Chambers/Team, Case Inquiry/Consultation desk, and Chamber address & contact details in Kochi (Cochin), Kerala.
- Tone & aesthetic: Dignified, authoritative, refined typography suitable for high-stakes litigation chambers.
